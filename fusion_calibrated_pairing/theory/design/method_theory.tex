\subsection{Prequential paired conditional return}
Let $a_j$ be the immutable complete-lease contrast predicted from a common
strong probabilistic forecaster, $h_j$ its physical-source contrasts,
and $U_j=g_j/N_j$ the originally issued complete-lease target. The reusable
observation is
\begin{equation}
 E_j=U_j-a_j,\qquad Z_j=h_j-a_j\mathbf1.
 \label{eq:prequential-paired-residual}
\end{equation}
The complete tuple enters state only after all lease labels mature. It
is never reprojected using a later model trained on those observations.
A dedicated state-fit library supplies persistent paired evidence across
physical-chain restarts; arrived local tuples supply subsequent adaptation.
Every information control uses the same strong source probabilities,
model-excluded prefix quality, immutable target population and execution
contract.

For locally weighted tuple atoms $(e_i,z_i)$, missing-coordinate uncertainty
$V_i$, and a common covariance kernel $K$, define
\begin{equation}
 \widehat f(e,z)=\sum_iw_i\varphi_{m+1}
 \bigl((e,z);\ell_i,V_i+K\bigr),\qquad
 \widehat p(u\mid h,a)\propto
 \mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(u)\widehat f(u-a,h-a\mathbf1).
 \label{eq:prequential-conditional-return}
\end{equation}
One paid score is
$S=N\operatorname{LT}_{1/2}(\widehat p)-5$.
The fixed tail mass and density bandwidth are shared by all controls.
The strong pair-factorized control uses
$f_F(e,z)=f_E(e)\prod_s f_{Z_s\mid E}(z_s\mid e)$, which preserves the
same residual marginal and every residual/source pair marginal before
current support restriction. The full Gaussian control preserves the
complete mean and covariance. A diagonal-kernel joint-density competitor
retains all tuple locations and is allowed to retain higher-order information.

\begin{proposition}[Prequential target integrity and affine return score]
Suppose every issued tuple is immutable and inserted only at its complete
maturity. Conditional on the common issued forecaster and model versions,
an issued fusion score at time $t$ is unchanged by modifications to complete
target fields whose maturity exceeds $t$. The common forecaster itself uses
only individually arrived labels. Thus its general causality statement
concerns labels not yet arrived, rather than every label of an unfinished
lease. If $U=a+E$, then
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{LT}_\alpha(U\mid h,a)
 =a+\operatorname{LT}_\alpha(E\mid h-a\mathbf1,a),
 \label{eq:prequential-tail-shift}
\end{equation}
with residual support $[-1-a,1-a]$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At issuance, the anchor and descriptor are fixed by the common issued
forecaster and model versions. The conditioning state depends only on the
permanent training/error prefix, the earlier completed library, and local
tuples with complete maturity at most the issuance time. Altering a later
complete-target field cannot change these arguments. Separately, an
unarrived individual label is absent from every model-update input.
For the second claim,
translation by $a$ preserves quantile order. The quantile integral defining
lower expected shortfall translates by the same constant; restricting the
residual to $[-1-a,1-a]$ maps exactly to physical return support $[-1,1]$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Calibration and the return connection.}
A disjoint score-calibration population stores immutable normalized errors
$r_i=(S_i-X_i)/N_i$, where $X_i=g_i-5$ is the conservative complete target.
A signed conditional empirical quantile $q(z)$ produces the single score
$L=\operatorname{clip}_{[-N-5,N-5]}\{S-Nq(z)\}$.
Let $b_j\in\{0,1\}$ denote actual admission after the common execution
guard, keeping $a_j$ for the probabilistic prediction anchor.
If $q(z)$ is a valid conditional probability-$p$ quantile of the future
normalized score error, then
$\Pr(X\geq L\mid z)\geq p$. Local empirical weighting alone does not
establish this assumption. For an admitted score $L>0$ and actual return
$D\geq X$, regardless of whether the conditional-risk model is valid,
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf1_{\{D<0\}}\leq\mathbf1_{\{X<L\}},\qquad
 (-D)_+\leq(L-X)_+,\qquad
 \sum_{j:b_j=1}D_j\geq
 \sum_{j:b_j=1}L_j-\sum_{j:b_j=1}(L_j-X_j)_+.
 \label{eq:prequential-calibration-return}
\end{equation}
These inequalities connect actual admission calibration and excess magnitude
to paid return. They neither certify the fitted quantile nor imply universal
positive gain. A shared reservation ledger supplies its separate deterministic
cumulative-loss guarantee for every controller.

\paragraph{Innovation boundary.}
Full temporal target/source pairing can encode decisions not determined by
individual target/source margins or complete second-order summaries.
The independent empirical claim is an additional paid action under the same
execution guarantee, established by all changed-action returns and complete
pipeline comparisons. Gaussian smoothing, Schur completion and expected
shortfall are established tools. Full non-Bayesian conditional-density
methods and tuple-aware adaptive matrices can retain the same information;
they must be evaluated as direct competitors rather than excluded by an
information restriction they do not satisfy.
