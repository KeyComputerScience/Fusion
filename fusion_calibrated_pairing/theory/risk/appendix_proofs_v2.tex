\subsection{Causal stratified calibration and its execution guarantee}
\label{app:immutable-calibration-v2}
This appendix describes the frozen second calibration version. The earlier
version and its results remain separately retained. Let $S_j$ be the raw
complete-lease paid score, $N_j\in(0,128]$ its request capacity, $z_j$ its
issued context, and $m_j$ its complete-feedback maturity. The previsible
stratum is $g_j=\mathbf1_{\{\text{current disagreement}_j>0\}}$.
Store $(S_j,N_j,z_j,g_j,m_j)$ at issuance. A completed counterfactual
target $X_j$ gives the immutable residual
\begin{equation}
 e_j=(S_j-X_j)/N_j.
 \label{eq:v2-immutable-residual}
\end{equation}
Only callbacks with $m_j$ at or before the current issuance enter the
online archive, whether their lease was admitted or rejected. A new model
version does not recompute an old score or its residual.

Let $\mathcal I_0$ be the complete canonical score-fitting library from
the physical partition disjoint from state fitting, configuration
selection and test. Each physical origin enters this library once.
Let $\mathcal I_j$ contain the globally latest 48 eligible matured online
callbacks, in callback-arrival order. First retain those 48 records and
then restrict a query to the current stratum; do not retain 48 records
per stratum. Define $\mathcal I_{0g}=\{i\in\mathcal I_0:g_i=g\}$ and
$\mathcal I_{jg}=\{i\in\mathcal I_j:g_i=g\}$. For
$K_b(z,z_i)=\exp\{-\|z-z_i\|^2/(2b^2)\}$, the query weights are
\begin{equation}
 w_{ji}=\mathbf1_{\{g_i=g_j\}}K_b(z_j,z_i)
 \begin{cases}
 2/|\mathcal I_{0g_j}|,&i\in\mathcal I_{0g_j},\\
 0.97^{a_j(i)},&i\in\mathcal I_{jg_j},
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:v2-stratified-calibration-weights}
\end{equation}
The first case is absent when its stratum is empty. Here $a_j(i)$ is
the number of subsequently retained callback arrivals, including arrivals
in the other stratum. Filtering does not rejuvenate an old record.
The persistent fitting library has conditional base mass two before
kernel weighting and is not assigned participant-local arrival ages.
For a nonempty query, $q_j$ is the first sorted residual whose cumulative
weight reaches $p\sum_iw_{ji}$. If the query is empty, set $q_j=2$.
The sole issued score is
\begin{equation}
 L_j=\operatorname{clip}(S_j-N_jq_j,-N_j-5,N_j-5).
 \label{eq:v2-conditional-paid-score}
\end{equation}
The implementation issues $L_j=-N_j-5$ explicitly in the empty case.
The nine shared configurations are
$b\in\{0.25,0.5,1\}$ and $p\in\{0.8,0.9,0.95\}$.
This is one score correction; it adds no separate statistical admission
gate. Readiness, $L_j>0$ and the common execution reserve determine
actual admission. All strata and all mature feedback remain recorded.

\begin{proposition}[Physical-support fallback]
\label{prop:v2-support-fallback}
Suppose $S_j,X_j\in[-N_j-5,N_j-5]$. The empty-stratum correction
$q_j=2$ satisfies $L_j\leq X_j$ for every possible complete outcome
and produces no positive paid proposal.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The physical support gives
$(S_j-X_j)/N_j\leq((N_j-5)-(-N_j-5))/N_j=2$.
Also $S_j-2N_j\leq-N_j-5$, so clipping gives
$L_j=-N_j-5\leq X_j$. Since $N_j>0$, this score is negative.
\end{proof}
This proposition concerns the empty query only. A nonempty locally
weighted quantile does not acquire a conditional coverage guarantee
from hard stratification or from its nominal probability $p$.
The report therefore records empty-query counts, suppressed positive
raw proposals and their potential complete returns; abstention is not
counted as an extra beneficial deployment.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional validity at actual admission]
\label{prop:v2-conditional-admission-validity}
Let $\mathcal F_j^-$ contain the complete pre-admission information,
including the fitted fusion law, all available history, calibration
configuration and state, visible stratum, service feasibility and ledger.
Suppose $S_j,N_j,q_j$ and the actual admission $a_j$ are measurable
with respect to this filtration, $N_j>0$, and
\begin{equation}
 \Pr\{e_j>q_j\mid\mathcal F_j^-\}\leq\tau.
 \label{eq:v2-full-conditional-quantile-condition}
\end{equation}
Then the score in Eq.~\eqref{eq:v2-conditional-paid-score} obeys
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E[a_j\mathbf1_{\{X_j<L_j\}}\mid\mathcal F_j^-]
 \leq\tau a_j.
 \label{eq:v2-actual-conditional-coverage}
\end{equation}
If admission requires $L_j>0$ and actual complete return satisfies
$D_j\geq X_j$, the same inequality holds with
$\mathbf1_{\{D_j<0\}}$ in place of the lower-target violation indicator.
Thus a predictable readiness or reserve restriction preserves a valid
full-information conditional bound at the actions it actually executes.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Before clipping, $X_j<S_j-N_jq_j$ is equivalent to $e_j>q_j$.
Upper clipping can only lower the score. Lower clipping sets it no
higher than the lower physical support, where a violation is impossible.
Consequently $\mathbf1_{\{X_j<L_j\}}\leq\mathbf1_{\{e_j>q_j\}}$.
Multiply by the $\mathcal F_j^-$-measurable indicator $a_j$ and take
conditional expectations. On an admitted harmful lease,
$X_j\leq D_j<0<L_j$, so its harmful indicator is bounded by its
lower-target violation indicator.
\end{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:v2-full-conditional-quantile-condition} is a
sufficient assumption, not a conclusion about the executed empirical
quantile. It conditions on all information used in selection and actual
admission, rather than only on the three-dimensional context or stratum.
Marginal calibration on all issued scores is insufficient for this
condition. Finite physical support, delayed callbacks and stratification
ensure causal implementation; they do not establish the condition in a
new participant or session. Actual-admission coverage and exceedance are
therefore measured directly.

\begin{proposition}[Pointwise score, loss and paid-return connection]
\label{prop:v2-calibration-return-link}
For every admitted lease with $L_j>0$ and $D_j\geq X_j$, define
$V_j=\mathbf1_{\{X_j<L_j\}}$ and $E_j=(L_j-X_j)_+$. Then
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf1_{\{D_j<0\}}\leq V_j,\qquad
 (-D_j)_+\leq E_j,\qquad
 \sum_{j:a_j=1}D_j\geq
 \sum_{j:a_j=1}L_j-\sum_{j:a_j=1}E_j.
 \label{eq:v2-pointwise-paid-return-link}
\end{equation}
These statements hold for every realized stream, including a stream
whose empirical calibration differs from its nominal target.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $D_j<0$, then $X_j\leq D_j<0<L_j$, which proves the indicator
inequality and $-D_j\leq-X_j<L_j-X_j$. If $D_j\geq0$, its
negative loss is zero. Finally
$D_j\geq X_j\geq L_j-(L_j-X_j)_+$ pointwise; sum over actual actions.
\end{proof}
In the evaluated contract $X_j=g_j^{\rm lease}-5$ and
$D_j=g_j^{\rm lease}-3-I_j$, where $I_j\in[0,2]$ counts correct
candidate requests lost to the additional admission interruption.
Hence $D_j-X_j=2-I_j\in[0,2]$. The full target and actual return
contain installation and restoration fees. Outside a nonoverlapping
lease the shared updating reference is restored, and identical common
preparation work cancels, so complete service increment is
$J-J_0=\sum_j a_jD_j$.

\begin{theorem}[Common pathwise paid-loss guarantee]
\label{thm:v2-pathwise-paid-loss}
For each declared history chain initialize settled negative loss
$C=0$ and total pending reservation $Q=0$. Admit a proposed lease only
if $C+Q+M_j\leq B$, adding its valid reservation $M_j$ atomically.
At complete-feedback maturity, atomically replace that reservation by
$(-D_j)_+\leq M_j$. Assume nonoverlapping paid leases, restoration,
the common-work contract above, and that every admitted lease's deficit
at each service and fee boundary is also at most $M_j$. Then for every
data stream and feedback delay schedule,
\begin{equation}
 C+Q\leq B,\qquad
 \sum_{j:a_j=1}(-D_j)_+\leq B,\qquad
 J(t)-J_0(t)\geq-B.
 \label{eq:v2-common-loss-budget}
\end{equation}
No calibration or posterior assumption is required. Under the evaluated
four-window, 32-request-cap contract, $M_j=130$ is a valid reservation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The invariant holds initially. Admission adds $M_j$ only after the
guard checks the invariant. Settlement changes $C+Q$ by
$(-D_j)_+-M_j\leq0$; no positive outcome refills the cumulative
negative-loss budget. Induction covers arbitrary admissible callback
orders and delays. After all targets mature, $Q=0$ and the second
inequality follows. At an intermediate physical service time, settled
leases contribute at least $-C$; every admitted but unsettled lease
contributes at least minus its reservation, even if its physically
completed target is still hidden by feedback delay. These contributions
sum to at least $-(C+Q)\geq-B$.

For the numerical reservation, an eligible four-window lease contains
at most 128 unit-reward requests and starts at a probe boundary with at
least one common dropped request. The shared reference therefore serves
at most 127 requests in that lease. At any request or fee boundary, loss
of all those reference rewards plus the two installation and one
restoration fee is at most $127+3=130$. Additional admission
interruptions are already included among the potentially lost served
reference rewards. This bounds both the complete return and every
physical prefix of the lease.
\end{proof}
The guarantee is shared by every fusion comparator; its independent
decision value is assessed under this identical guard. It applies to
each declared history chain. Resetting $K$ independent ledgers permits
an aggregate negative-loss bound $KB$, not $B$. An application with
different request caps, rewards, fees or common-interruption timing
must derive a new valid complete and prefix reservation.

\paragraph{Prespecified per-chain statistical report.}
The separate reporting script uses the declared nine policies, every
reported session--delay history chain, four bounded endpoints and a
fixed six-value exponential grid. It releases a time-uniform upper
envelope only for the longest fully matured origin-ordered admission
prefix; missing suffix endpoints receive their worst-case support.
The endpoints are target violation, harmful return,
$(L_j-X_j)_+/256$ and $(-D_j)_+/130$.
Each endpoint lies in $[0,1]$ under the stated contract. Its statistical
target is the mean latent conditional endpoint risk of that executed
prefix, using the enlarged causal filtration that includes physically
completed earlier leases even before learner feedback. This report
does not change the score, certify an arbitrary next environment, or
count repeated delay histories as independent physical sites.
