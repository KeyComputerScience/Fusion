\paragraph{Conditional calibration of the single paid score.}
The paired conditional law supplies a raw lower-tail score
$S_j=N_j\operatorname{LT}_{0.5}(\widehat P_j)-5$.
We store its issued value and common context
$z_j=(a_j,\mathrm{disagreement}_j,\mathrm{active\ fraction}_j)$.
Only after the complete counterfactual target $X_j$ matures do we form
$e_j=(S_j^{\rm issued}-X_j)/N_j$. The canonical score-fitting library
is persistent with total mass two; the online calibration archive keeps
the latest 48 mature residuals with discount 0.97. Context kernels and
the same nine bandwidth/quantile configurations are shared by every
information control. The calibrated score and paid proposal are
\begin{equation}
 q_j=\widehat Q_{p,b}(e\mid z_j),\qquad
 L_j=\operatorname{clip}_{[-N_j-5,N_j-5]}(S_j-N_jq_j),\qquad
 a_j=\mathbf1_{\{\mathrm{ready}_j,L_j>0,\mathrm{reserve}_j\}}.
 \label{eq:conditional-immutable-score}
\end{equation}
The signed correction can repair both underforecast and overforecast.
There is one calibrated score and the common Fixed130 execution contract.
Rejected leases provide the same completely matured potential feedback
as admitted ones. This feedback contract is shared across all controls;
it is not bandit feedback. Weighted empirical quantiles are evaluated
through actual admissions, including coverage and exceedance magnitude.
They do not automatically give conditional coverage under participant
shift or adaptive law fitting. The sufficient statistical assumptions
and complete risk proofs are stated in the appendix.
