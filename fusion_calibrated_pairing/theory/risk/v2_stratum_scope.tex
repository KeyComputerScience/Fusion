\paragraph{Previsible calibration strata.}
The final calibration revision uses the binary, pre-outcome stratum
$g_j=\mathbf1_{\{\mathrm{current\ disagreement}_j>0\}}$.
Every complete counterfactual callback is still retained. When querying
the conditional empirical quantile, both the persistent score-fitting
library and the online archive are restricted to $g_i=g_j$. Quiet
forecast residuals therefore cannot change the informative stratum's
quantile through their residual values. The same nine configurations,
raw conditional law, paid score and service guard are used in every arm.
The persistent fitting distribution has conditional mass two; online
records retain their actual callback-arrival age. No independent-subject
count is inferred from the weighted effective count.

If no same-stratum feedback is available, set $q_j=2$ and issue the
physical lower score $L_j=-N_j-5$. Indeed,
$S_j\leq N_j-5$ and $X_j\geq-N_j-5$ imply
$(S_j-X_j)/N_j\leq2$, so the support fallback has no lower-target
violation and makes no positive paid proposal. This is the same single
score's support fallback, not a second admission gate. Count and report
empty-stratum occurrences, suppressed positive raw proposals and their
actual beneficial/harmful potential returns; abstention is not reported
as an additional beneficial deployment.

For nonempty strata, signed empirical correction remains subject to
finite-sample error, participant dependence and changing conditional laws.
Hard stratification removes a stated population mismatch; it does not
establish nominal conditional coverage. The sufficient-quantile proposition
and the time-uniform observed-admission risk report retain their original
assumptions. Version 1, its frozen source hash and all development outcomes
remain separately available. The revision must be frozen before acquiring
the new physical raw data to qualify as that collection's prospective
method; an already acquired test cannot be relabelled prospective.
