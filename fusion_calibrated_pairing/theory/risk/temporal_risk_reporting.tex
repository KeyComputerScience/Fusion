\subsection{Prespecified temporal risk reporting for actual admissions}
\label{app:temporal-admission-risk-report}
This reporting calculation does not change the fusion score or admission.
The selected scoring configuration for each pipeline is fixed before its
test replay. Index actual admitted leases in origin order within each
declared history chain. Let $\mathcal H_i^-$ contain the complete fitting
and selection information, all physically completed preceding lease
outcomes, current issued inputs and the current execution state. Past
complete outcomes may belong to this enlarged mathematical filtration
before their labels are released to the learner. Nonoverlapping leases
make the actual action and score predictable in $\mathcal H_i^-$, while
the current complete target is a later outcome. The live controller still
uses only mature callbacks; the enlarged filtration is used solely to
define the statistical target.

For each actual admission, report the four bounded endpoints
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 Y_i^{\rm viol}&=\mathbf1_{\{X_i<L_i\}},&
 Y_i^{\rm harm}&=\mathbf1_{\{D_i<0\}},\\
 Y_i^{\rm exc}&=(L_i-X_i)_+/256,&
 Y_i^{\rm loss}&=(-D_i)_+/130.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:admitted-risk-four-endpoints}
\end{equation}
These belong to $[0,1]$: $N_i\leq128$, $L_i\leq N_i-5$,
$X_i\geq-N_i-5$ and the actual-return contract gives $D_i\geq-130$.
For one endpoint let
$r_i=\mathbb E[Y_i\mid\mathcal H_i^-]$. No independence among
participants, sessions, methods or delayed replays is required for the
following per-chain bound. Its target is the mean conditional endpoint
risk of the already executed admission prefix, rather than an individual
future lease or a new environment.

\begin{proposition}[Declared-family time-uniform upper envelope]
\label{prop:declared-temporal-risk-envelope}
Declare $K=9$ selected pipelines, $C$ reported history chains, $E=4$
endpoints, $J=6$ values
$\Lambda=\{0.25,0.5,1,2,4,8\}$, and family error $\delta=0.05$.
Simultaneously for every declared pipeline, chain, endpoint and admission
prefix $n\geq1$, with probability at least $1-\delta$,
\begin{equation}
 \frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n r_i\leq
 U_n:=\min\!\left\{1,\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
 \frac{\lambda\sum_{i=1}^nY_i+
       \log(KCEJ/\delta)}{(1-e^{-\lambda})n}\right\}.
 \label{eq:declared-temporal-risk-envelope}
\end{equation}
Here $C$ is the complete declared session/block--delay reporting inventory,
including chains without admissions. It counts the family inspected and
does not count independent physical environments. The bound reported
separately for each collection uses that collection's declared $C$;
a simultaneous multi-collection claim instead allocates to their combined
chain inventory or explicitly divides $\delta$ between collections.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $c_\lambda=1-e^{-\lambda}$. Convexity gives
$e^{-\lambda y}\leq1-c_\lambda y$ for $y\in[0,1]$.
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 M_{n,\lambda}=\exp\left\{
 c_\lambda\sum_{i=1}^n r_i-\lambda\sum_{i=1}^nY_i\right\}
 \label{eq:admitted-risk-supermartingale}
\end{equation}
is a nonnegative supermartingale with initial value one, since each
conditional multiplier is at most
$e^{c_\lambda r_i}(1-c_\lambda r_i)\leq1$.
Ville's inequality bounds a threshold crossing at any $n$ by
$\delta/(KCEJ)$. A union bound over the declared $KCEJ$ processes
permits both the minimum over the fixed exponential grid and arbitrary
admission stopping. Rearranging each threshold gives
Eq.~\eqref{eq:declared-temporal-risk-envelope}.
\end{proof}

With delayed labels, release this bound on the longest \emph{fully
matured origin prefix} of actual admissions. An out-of-order set of
arrived outcomes is not substituted for the prefix. If $n$ admissions
form that prefix and $m$ subsequent admitted endpoints remain outside it,
their support gives the upper bound
$(nU_n+m)/(n+m)$ for the full executed prefix. At $n=0$ the latter
bound is one when an admission is pending; an empty admission set has
no defined coverage rate. The calculation remains valid if maturity
depends on outcomes, because the event is uniform over all origin prefixes.

\paragraph{Empirical calibration and the scope of its risk claim.}
The tables report actual coverage numerators and denominators, exceedance
magnitudes $(L_i-X_i)_+$, negative returns, negative-loss totals and
ledger refusals alongside the per-chain envelope. Pooling records across
delay schedules is descriptive; it does not create additional independent
sites or justify a stopped-sample binomial interval.
An adaptive weighted empirical quantile with nominal probability $p$
does not by itself imply
$\Pr\{(S_i-X_i)/N_i>q_i\mid\mathcal F_i^-\}\leq1-p$
for the full pre-admission filtration. The gate $L_i>0$, the selected
configuration and ledger state can change the relevant conditional
population, while residual dependence and finite support create further
estimation error. Hard disagreement strata remove the specified mixing
of quiet and informative residuals; they do not prove this conditional
inequality. Physical-support validity of the empty-stratum fallback and
the pathwise loss budget are separate deterministic guarantees.
The statistical envelope quantifies the observed admitted-prefix evidence
without assuming the empirical quantile already attains its nominal risk.
