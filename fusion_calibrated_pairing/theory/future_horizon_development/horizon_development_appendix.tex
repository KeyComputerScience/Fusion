\section{Retained horizon-state development and score-monotonicity refinement}
\label{app:horizon-development}
This is used-data development, separate from frozen CJRT-P and the new
physical-collection protocol. No OPPORTUNITY observation enters this work.
The old selfBACK participants and former held-out outcomes had already been
inspected. One horizon-state candidate H-v0 was declared before its new
calibration/evaluation runs; one H-v1 score refinement was subsequently
recorded before its replay. Both versions, every nine-arm selection and all

\subsection{One physical-source path state}
For original issue window $k$, let $p^C_{k,i},p^R_{k,i}$ be the available
candidate/reference probabilities, and define
\begin{equation}
 t_k=n_k^{-1}\sum_i\tfrac12\|p^C_{k,i}-p^R_{k,i}\|_1,\quad
 a_k^{\rm soft}=n_k^{-1}\sum_i p^{\rm PDF}_{k,i}\cdot(p^C_{k,i}-p^R_{k,i}),\quad
 \nu_{k,s}=n_k^{-1}\sum_i p_{k,s,i}\cdot(p^C_{k,i}-p^R_{k,i})-a_k^{\rm soft}.
 \label{eq:h-development-soft-evidence}
\end{equation}
Let $m_{k,s}$ be the mean top-two probability margin of physical source $s$.
Its single six-coordinate block is
$\Phi_{j,s}=((\nu_{j,s},m_{j,s}),
(\nu_{j-1,s},m_{j-1,s}),(\nu_{j-2,s},m_{j-2,s}))$.
These are current and preceding ordinal issuance windows; they are not
invented independent sources or asserted contiguous clock-duration segments.
A new recording resets the path. Startup and unavailable source/lag
coordinates remain explicitly unobserved. Stored snapshots keep their
original issued model versions, not future-model historical reprojections.
The shared eight-coordinate $W_j$ contains the hard and soft PDF anchors,
current C/R probability margins, hard disagreement, $t_j$, active fraction
and startup-history fraction. The target remains original full-lease hard
correctness return: $E_j=U_j-A_j$. Thus the predictor and full-lease outcome
have distinct temporal supports.

One working joint mixture models $(E,W,\Phi_1,\ldots,\Phi_m)$ and conditions
on the present observed configuration. The history weight is
$\exp(-\|W_j-W_i\|^2)\rho_i$, with
$\rho_i=\max(r_i,t_i)$ and discount $0.97^{(k_j-k_i)_+}$ for local records.
This keeps soft-informative quiet origins. A canonical state-fit library,
48 matured local tuples, the shared prefix quality and Gaussian missing-block
completion remain fixed. The prior mass is two; covariance scales are 0.01
for residual/common coordinates and $0.01\bar q/q_s$ for all six coordinates
of each physical-source block. Scott smoothing and lower tail mass $1/2$
are fixed. The common-only control uses $P(E\mid W)$, so it is a strong
predictable-context control, not a context-free unconditional baseline.
The factorized control preserves each complete multivariate
$(E,\Phi_s)\mid W$ margin:
\begin{equation}
 f_{\rm factor}(e\mid W,\Phi)\propto
 \frac{\prod_s f(e,\Phi_s\mid W)}{f(e\mid W)^{\bar m-1}},
 \label{eq:h-development-physical-factor}
\end{equation}
where $\bar m$ counts physical-source blocks with observed path coordinates.
Full Gaussian, full joint diagonal-kernel KDE and postconditional
maximum-entropy controls receive the same complete descriptor and library.
The latter receives mean/variance after full paired conditioning.

The state/score/selection participants are respectively
$\{42,43,44,46\}$, $\{47,48\}$ and $\{49,50\}$; all 13 already-used
participants 51--63 form development evaluation. Canonical delay 151001
supplies state/score origins once. Selection uses delays 151001--151003;
evaluation uses 152001--152005. All nine pipelines receive exactly nine
$(b,p)$ choices, $b\in\{.25,.5,1\}$ and $p\in\{.8,.9,.95\}$, with the
same signed immutable-score calibration in H-v0. Equal primary budget and
reservation are 130. PDF/QMF retain their active-objective tabular source
models; DBF remains the disclosed operator adaptation. Source learning rate
0.2 is a fixed known-data design choice, not a new backbone search.
QMF is the strongest complete controller selected from calibration in both
versions. Five delay arrangements reuse the same physical recordings.

\subsection{One monotone score and its exact boundary}
H-v1 changes only $q^+=\max(0,\widehat q)$ in the same paid score. The raw
laws, immutable callbacks and counterfactual feedback population are
unchanged. \begin{proposition}[Monotone preservation of the raw paid tail]
\label{prop:h-development-monotone}
For a fixed configuration and counterfactual history with raw score inside
physical support,
\begin{equation}
 L^+=\operatorname{clip}(S-Nq^+)\leq
 L^{\rm signed}=\operatorname{clip}(S-N\widehat q),\qquad L^+\leq S.
 \label{eq:h-development-monotone-score}
\end{equation}
If, for the complete pre-admission filtration $\mathcal F_j$,
$\Pr(X_j<L_j^{\rm signed}\mid\mathcal F_j)\leq\alpha_j$, then
$\Pr(X_j<L_j^+\mid\mathcal F_j)\leq\alpha_j$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The support clip is nondecreasing, $q^+\geq\widehat q$, and the raw score
is inside the physical support. Hence a positive H-v1 proposal requires a
positive raw tail score. This is one score functional, not another guard.
If the original signed correction satisfies the complete-filtration
conditional violation condition, $\{X_j<L_j^+\}\subseteq\{X_j<L_j^{\rm signed}\}$,
so conditional probability monotonicity proves the claim.
Empirical quantiles do not automatically satisfy the premise.
\end{proof}
The proposition is pointwise: changing the selected configuration or
saving budget for later actions does not imply global action nesting or
positive net return.

H-v1 recalibration selects $b=1,p=.8$ for paired instead of H-v0's

\paragraph{Mechanism audit boundary.}
Previsible information survival passes: soft differences remain on 180
hard-quiet forks. Actual profitable quiet recovery does not pass: no H-v0
quiet admission is beneficial. Physical-block dependence has a local action
and complete gain witness, including the shared-correction check and all
four value totals. Tail-shape necessity does not pass: postconditional
moments have identical actions. Comparison with the calibration-selected
QMF pipeline has a positive mean in H-v0; comprehensive superiority does
not pass because common-only is stronger. Nonnegative calibration proves
pointwise score monotonicity; its independently reselected complete pipeline
does not preserve H-v0 value. The common budget audit passes, including
H-v1's actual refusals. No result establishes new-collection confirmation.
