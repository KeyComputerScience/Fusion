# What the new bound can establish

The new manuscript fragment derives an exact diagnostic for the declared residual-law intervention. The exact posterior response, score response and crossing condition connect retained historical pairing to the same complete paid target and existing gate. The exact-marginal bridge fixes all source marginal densities, including their locations and uncertainties. The moment-matched Gaussian bridge fixes the full residual mean and covariance. A bridge response under these controls therefore localizes the fitted-law mechanism more sharply than a general information-order statement.

The variance/TV bound is a finite perturbation result for two posterior distributions on the same bounded target. It is valid for arbitrary such distributions and improves the elementary 5δ estimate to min(1,4δ). It does not depend on a Gaussian assumption. Its square-root expression is also sharper than the loose alternatives sqrt(4δ) and 4δ/(2ν).

TV between two fitted laws is observable once those laws and the current forecast are fixed. It measures sensitivity to the chosen information reduction. It is not evidence that either law approximates the true physical conditional target distribution. Thus the new theorem cannot turn estimated RSS calibration into an assumption-free true-law guarantee. One must supply a separately justified bound to the true posterior, based on a stated statistical model, held-out calibration method, or distributional assumption.

If such a true-posterior TV bound is available, the score bound propagates it to an action margin at the same origin, with the same N, q, ν, opportunities and execution state. The theorem protects the fitted gate sign when that margin exceeds Kδ. The algebra does not establish realized positive return for an admitted action, conditional lower-score coverage, or total utility for a sequential policy with different future reserve states.

For example, under a correctly specified common-prior residual generative model, define the nonnegative line likelihoods L(u) and Lhat(u), their positive finite integrals e and ehat, and A = ∫[-1,1] |Lhat(u)−L(u)| du. Direct normalization gives

TV(phat,p) ≤ (A+|ehat−e|)/(2 max(ehat,e)) ≤ A/max(ehat,e).

This is a conditional density-estimation route to the posterior-TV certificate; A concerns the unknown true likelihood and is not supplied by fitted joint/product agreement or marginal agreement. A global residual-density L1 guarantee alone does not control every observed target line without further regularity or a probabilistic qualification. Small evidence can also make conditional inference unstable.

The worst-case TV score bound can be conservative. In the accompanying synthetic zero-disagreement exact-marginal→joint diagnostic, δ=0.5, the actual score change is about 25.09 but Kδ is about 290.41 at N=128, q=1.2815515655 and ν=0.01. The exact bridge identity is therefore the useful mechanism calculation for those endpoints; the worst-case bound should not be presented as a strong empirical RSS action certificate. A small independently certified posterior perturbation and a large paid-score margin are needed for the latter interpretation.

The contribution here is the manuscript-specific analytical connection between retained residual-law structure, complete-target posterior moments and its paid gate, plus an explicit perturbation margin. Affine density mixing and the laws of total variance are standard identities. This is a diagnostic/proof addition, not a new fusion optimizer or a generic value-of-information theorem relabeled as a new algorithm.
