## Required restrictions

1. **Correct horizon coupling.** If errors are generated by current state `H_k` and stakes by future state `H_(k+l)`, the joint moment is `sum_h gamma_k(h) d_(h,l) R_h`, assuming conditional independence of current residual and future transition path given current state. `sum_h gamma_future(h) B_h^2 R_h` associates the wrong error regime unless the forecast itself targets the future horizon. If `B_h` is learned directly as a horizon-integrated conditional-current-state slope, use it directly and call it that empirical surrogate; do not apply an extra transition transport without explaining the semantics.

2. **State-local scalar factorization.** `B_h^2 R_h` assumes deterministic declared `B_h` per state. For random stakes correlated with residuals within a state, the proper object is `E[B^2 e e^T | H=h, F]`. Replacing it by `E[B^2|h] E[e e^T|h]` requires conditional independence. A finite paired SGD improvement is not the loss sensitivity derivative automatically; a fitted value slope can be the declared surrogate.

3. **Information for weights.** The full-rank residual predictive model is conditional on history/current context and preforecast instrument status. It cannot simultaneously be an exact residual distribution conditional on the entire already-observed current vector `p`: then `e=p-y*1` is supported on a line. Define a preforecast information field for weights, freeze the weights, then use current `p` in the action rule. The controller's full information may be larger, and empirical moment risk cannot be silently treated as its conditional confidence interval.

4. **Bayesian claim scope.** Forward filtering/replay is exact under fixed correctly specified likelihood parameters. Frozen prefix Gaussian emissions with a bounded-error instrument are a working likelihood. Adaptive PSD moments use residual-fitted responsibilities and are empirical plug-ins, not a conjugate normal–inverse-Wishart posterior. Parameters should remain fixed during a replay operation. A fixed-lag replay is exact only with sufficiently bounded delays and a completed boundary message; too-old arrivals require a retained earlier origin or logged approximation/drop.

5. **Mask meaning.** No error coordinate can be invented. Mask-qualified state archives are PSD but generally identify a mask-conditioned observed population. Ignorable masking conditional on declared state/context must be stated if this is transported to unconditional active-source risk. Arbitrary informative missingness is not corrected by a kernel or a Gaussian marginal.

6. **Fixed archive correction.** The finite Hoeffding lemma deliberately applies only to independent bounded vectors and fixed prespecified weights independent of outcomes. It does NOT apply to residual-fitted posterior responsibilities, Markov dependence, or future-context kernels correlated with outcomes. Its explicit prior term, local distribution bias, and finite effective support replace the old erroneous rolling-archive almost-sure convergence. Growing untruncated equal-weight iid archives with a finite prior have the separate strong-law result; fixed capacity and fixed beta<1 do not in general.

7. **Core benefit scope.** The exact guarantee is weighted prediction risk `E[B^2 (w'e)^2]`, and a risk gap robust to operator-norm error, inside the declared linear combination class. Latent stakes add an additional term to realized utility prediction; the squared-stake matrix alone does not prove Bayes-optimal action selection or positive net service return. The conditional admission theorem assumes valid simultaneous full-horizon value allowances; empirical validation quantiles are reported as empirical allowances, with actual return measured.

8. **Positive-stakes theorem.** Uniform shared posterior, three states with the same marginal variance and a different correlated pair, and unequal strictly positive state importance `(a,b,b)` give a strictly improved consequential risk under weighted fusion versus both unweighted and trace-matched controls. All regularizer terms minimize at uniform, while the weighted moment's tangent gradient does not vanish. A short complete proof is now in the fragment; it avoids the earlier zero-stakes shortcut. This is a mechanism theorem, not measured SGD net return. Trace normalizing the weighted matrix changes its positive scale but preserves this proof.

## Additional deterministic transport sensitivity

For row-stochastic `P` and `P_tilde`, telescoping yields

`||P^l B^2 - P_tilde^l B_tilde^2||_infty <= ||B^2-B_tilde^2||_infty + l*||P-P_tilde||_infty*||B_tilde^2||_infty`.

Here the matrix infinity norm is the maximum absolute row sum. This separates stake calibration error and transition persistence error, with no iid assumptions. It may be inserted after the posterior moment decomposition if space permits.
