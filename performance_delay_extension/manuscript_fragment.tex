Relative to joint fusion, full fusion reduced harmful admissions by an average
of $1.0$, $3.0$ and $2.2$ on Occupancy864, MHEALTH and HAR, respectively;
Occupancy357 had no admissions under any of these four controllers.
The MHEALTH posterior-weight increment comprised suppression of the same
$k=4$ lease in four delay replays, each avoiding a realized loss of $22$;
the fifth replay was tied. The shared posterior mean and gate were retained,
and the weight change lowered the estimated gain from $11.609$ to $7.983$,
moving the gate score from $0.248$ to $-3.301$. The two HAR differences from
the block gate occurred at $k=68$ in seeds 72007 and 72010. Identical weights
and gains were used, but $U=B/(A+1)$ with $A=21.488$ and $21.692$ produced
posterior directional standard deviations of $3.018$ and $2.905$, compared
with block deviations of $13.026$ and $12.489$. The separately initialized,
causally updated thresholds yielded posterior penalties $5.489$ and $5.428$
versus block penalties $8.335$ and $8.615$. Each additional admitted lease
returned $17$ after its actual service loss and fees.

The extension also retains adverse outcomes. Full fusion still lost $22.0$
relative to the common refreshed-reference trajectory on MHEALTH
($95\%$ interval $[-38.96,-5.04]$), and tied the block gate in mean return,
with three positive and two negative paired differences.
Its pooled overall/informative coverage was $68.57\%/24.00\%$
($72/105$ and $6/25$ issued forks), far below the nominal
$90\%$ target; the MHEALTH benefit therefore cannot be interpreted as a
successful coverage guarantee. Full pooled informative coverage was $100\%$
($4/4$), $0\%$ ($0/5$) and $96.43\%$ ($27/28$) on Occupancy357,
Occupancy864 and HAR, respectively. These ratios pool issued forks across
the five delay replays; they differ from an unweighted average of per-seed rates.
Occupancy864's favorable aggregate coverage ($96.77\%$) coexisted with zero
coverage on its informative episodes. These observations support specific
action changes under retained controllers while limiting any general claim
of Bayesian superiority or reliable interval coverage.
